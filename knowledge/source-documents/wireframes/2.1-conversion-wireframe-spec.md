## STRATEGIC FOUNDATION

### Conversion Architecture Blueprint

**PRIMARY CONVERSION GOAL:** Generate qualified consultation requests from rural property owners in Bragg Creek, Rocky View County, Bearspaw, and Water Valley for interior finishing, exterior finishing/repairs, decking, and phased property improvements.

**Primary CTA:** Request a Consultation
**Secondary CTA:** View Our Work
**Soft CTA:** Talk Through Your Project

**Target performance benchmarks:**
- Homepage consultation conversion target: 3–6% of qualified local traffic
- Project gallery engagement target: 35–50% of homepage visitors click into gallery or project previews
- Contact form completion target: 40–60% of visitors who start the form
- Mobile parity target: mobile conversion rate should reach at least 80% of desktop conversion rate

### Arrival Context

Visitors are likely cautious, established rural homeowners. Their emotional state is not "buy now." It is closer to: "Can I trust this person on my property?" The website needs to reduce uncertainty, show proof, and make the consultation feel respectful rather than sales-driven.

### Value Proposition Hierarchy

**Hero Statement, 35 characters:** Trusted renovations for rural homes

**Supporting Pillar 1 — Trust:** One contractor, one relationship, fewer handoffs.
**Supporting Pillar 2 — Craftsmanship:** Interior finishing, exterior repairs, and decking handled with care.
**Supporting Pillar 3 — Rural Property Fit:** Built for homeowners who value land, privacy, durability, and long-term improvements.

The brand's strongest strategic position is "trusted, hands-on renovation support for rural property owners who value quality, continuity, and respect," with the mission line "The experience of quality. The quality of experience." acting as the emotional backbone of the website.

## User Psychology Profile

**Visitor Mental State:** The visitor is not looking for the loudest contractor. They are looking for the right-fit contractor. They want to feel that Haven Creek is steady, careful, compatible, and respectful before they share project details.

**Pain Severity Level:** Medium to high.
**Urgency Factors:** Seasonal exterior work, aging decks, unfinished interiors, repairs before weather changes, long-delayed property improvements.
**Trust Requirements:** Real work examples, clear service areas, simple language, calm design, no inflated claims, no hard-sell tactics.
**Decision-Making Style:** Slow, proof-led, relational.
**Objection Patterns:** "Will they respect my property?" "Will I deal with too many different people?" "Is this too small or too phased for them?" "Will they understand rural homes?" "Will the work feel complete and last?"
**Psychological Triggers:** Confidence, calm authority, continuity, privacy, craftsmanship, proof, long-term relationship value. The strongest conversion strategy is not urgency; it is confidence-building.

## WIREFRAME SPECIFICATIONS

### Above-the-Fold Architecture

**Desktop Layout:** Left side: logo/navigation, headline, subheadline, CTA pair, trust microcopy. Right side: large real project image showing interior finishing detail, finished deck, or exterior work in rural context.

**Mobile Layout:** Logo top, hamburger navigation, headline, subheadline, CTA, then image. Do not push the CTA below the first mobile viewport.

**HEADLINE:** "Trusted renovations for rural homes" (35 characters)
**SUBHEADLINE (74 characters):** "Hands-on finishing, repairs, and decks from planning to completion."

**CTA Row:** Primary `Request a Consultation`. Secondary `View Our Work`.
**CTA microcopy:** "No pressure. Just a clear conversation about your property."

**VISUAL SPECIFICATION:** Show outcome, not process. Honest, premium, and real. Avoid stock-looking luxury interiors.

**Fallback if no strong project image exists:** Warm off-white hero with logo monogram watermark, subtle green line motif, three small proof cards: Interior Finishing, Exterior Repairs, Decking.

### Primary Conversion Elements

**CTA Specifications:** Primary CTA text `Request a Consultation`. Button style: deep natural green background, off-white text, rounded but not pill-shaped. Secondary CTA: text link or outlined button, View Our Work.

**Friction-reducing microcopy:** "Custom quotes based on your property, scope, and timeline."

**Urgency/scarcity:** Do not use fake urgency. Avoid "limited spots," "book now," or "claim your quote."

**Trust Accelerators:** Place one short trust line under the CTA: "Serving Bragg Creek, Rocky View County, Bearspaw, and Water Valley." Three proof chips under hero image: One trusted contractor / Rural property respect / Design to completion.

## Persuasion Sequence Architecture

### Problem Agitation Module

**Section headline:** Renovation work should feel as solid as the result.

**Pain bullets (max 4):**
1. Too many handoffs can make the project feel scattered.
2. Rural properties require extra respect for privacy, access, land, and surroundings.
3. Small details decide whether the work feels complete or pieced together.
4. Many homeowners need phased improvements, not a one-time transactional job.

**Emotional amplification:** "When the wrong contractor is on the property, the stress is not just about the work. It is about comfort, privacy, trust, and whether the project will be handled properly."

**Transition to solution:** "Haven Creek Renovations was built for homeowners who want a more personal, hands-on way to improve their property."

### Solution Revelation Framework

**Section headline:** One contractor. One relationship. A clearer path from idea to completion.

**Mechanism in 3 steps:**
1. **Conversation** — Understand the property, priorities, timeline, and whether the work is one project or part of a longer plan.
2. **Planning** — Clarify scope, materials, design considerations, and practical project realities.
3. **Hands-On Completion** — Complete the work with attention to fit, finish, durability, and final details.

### Feature-Benefit Bridge

| Feature | Visitor Outcome | Required Proof |
|---|---|---|
| Hands-on from planning to completion | Fewer handoffs, clearer accountability | Process section + About page |
| Interior finishing | Spaces feel properly completed | Close-up detail photos |
| Exterior finishing and repairs | Property feels protected and maintained | Before/after images |
| Decking | Better outdoor living and daily property use | Wide deck/property shots |
| Rural property focus | Visitor feels understood | Service-area copy + rural imagery |
| Phased project support | Homeowner can plan improvements over time | Contact form field + follow-up messaging |

Interior finishing should receive the most visual and content priority because it is the flagship service.

## Social Proof Deployment

### Project Gallery as Primary Proof Engine

The project gallery should not be a simple image grid. Each project card needs: project type, general location, scope, challenge, finished result, and a "why it mattered" note.

**Featured proof modules:**
1. Hero proof chips — immediate reassurance.
2. Homepage gallery preview — 3 featured projects.
3. Project detail cards — scope, challenge, result.
4. About page trust section — working style and values.
5. Contact page reassurance block — what happens after submitting the form.

**Testimonial Strategy:** If testimonials exist, use 3–5. Each should be outcome-focused with name, location, project type, and specific praise around trust, communication, workmanship, or property respect. **If testimonials do not exist yet:** Use "project proof testimonials" — short captions written around the project outcome, without pretending they are client quotes.

**Trust Building Sequence:**
- **Immediate trust:** service area, calm hero, logo presence, simple navigation.
- **Emotional trust:** full-circle approach, property respect language, owner/working-style story.
- **Logical trust:** project gallery, scope details, before/after images, consultation process.
- **Risk reversal:** custom quote language, no-pressure consultation, clear form expectations.

## Objection Handling System

**Primary concern:** "Can I trust this contractor on my property?" → About section, property-respect copy, real work imagery, calm contact process.
**Secondary:** "Will there be too many people involved?" → "One contractor. One relationship." section.
**Secondary:** "Is my project too small or phased?" → contact form asks "Is this part of phased improvements over time?"
**Secondary:** "How much will this cost?" → quote-based pricing explanation.

**Risk Reversal Copy:** "Every property and project is different. The first step is a clear conversation about your goals, scope, and timing so we can determine whether Haven Creek Renovations is the right fit."

**Pricing Psychology Framework:** Do not create a pricing table. Use custom quote framework: "Custom quotes based on property needs, project scope, material choices, and timeline." Add a small "Why custom quotes?" microcopy on Contact or Services page.

## TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

### Responsive Behavior

**Mobile-First Requirements:** Critical first-screen elements: logo, headline, subheadline, primary CTA, service area line. Touch targets: minimum 44x44px. Sticky mobile CTA: optional after 40% scroll, text `Request Consultation`. Navigation: simple hamburger with Home, About, Services, Project Gallery, Service Areas, Contact.

**Tablet Requirements:** Two-column sections only where content has enough room.

**Desktop Requirements:** Max content width: 1180–1280px. Hero width: full viewport with contained content. Generous spacing, calm grids, no clutter.

### Performance Requirements

**Critical Path:** Load first: logo, headline, CTA, hero image, navigation. Delay: project gallery images below fold, map embeds, noncritical animations.

**Image Rules:** Use compressed WebP/AVIF. Real project images over stock. Lazy-load gallery images after the first visible row.

**Progressive Reveal:** Subtle fade-up only. No dramatic motion. Animation duration: 180–280ms. Avoid parallax unless extremely restrained.

**Fallback States:** Image loading: warm neutral skeleton block. Form submitting: button changes to `Sending...`. Form success: inline confirmation + thank-you page redirect. Form error: calm inline message, not red-heavy or alarming.

## INTERACTION SPECIFICATIONS

### Micro-Interactions

**Buttons:** Default deep green fill. Hover: slightly darker green, 2px upward lift, soft shadow. Active: return to baseline, darker pressed state. Disabled: muted green-gray, no shadow.

**Text Links:** Underline appears on hover. Color: charcoal default, deep green hover.

**Service Cards:** Hover: border shifts from warm gray to green, image gently scales 1.02x. Mobile: no hover dependency; full card is tappable.

**Gallery Cards:** Hover: reveal project type, location, and `View Project`. Mobile: captions always visible.

**Form Fields:** Focus: green outline, soft background shift. Error: short plain-language error beneath field. Success: green check icon with confirmation text.

## User Flow Paths

**PRIMARY PATH:**
1. Visitor lands on homepage.
2. Understands service and rural fit in hero.
3. Sees trust promise.
4. Reviews services.
5. Views project gallery preview.
6. Reads full-circle approach.
7. Clicks `Request a Consultation`.
8. Completes conversational form.
9. Lands on thank-you page with next steps.

**ALTERNATIVE PATHS:**
- Service-specific: Homepage → Services → Interior Finishing / Exterior Repairs / Decking → Contact.
- Proof-led: Homepage → Project Gallery → Project Detail → Contact.
- Local SEO: Bragg Creek / Rocky View / Bearspaw / Water Valley page → Project Gallery → Contact.

**ESCAPE ROUTES:** Visitors not ready to contact should be guided to `View Our Work`, `How We Work`, or a specific service page.

**Reading level:** Grade 7–9. Simple, direct, confident.

## TESTING ROADMAP

**Primary test variables:**
1. Hero headline: A: Trusted renovations for rural homes / B: One trusted contractor for your rural property
2. CTA language: A: Request a Consultation / B: Talk Through Your Project
3. Hero visual: A: interior finishing detail / B: exterior/decking rural property image
4. Proof placement: A: project gallery preview before services / B: gallery after full-circle approach
5. Contact form length: A: full qualification form / B: shorter form with project description field

**Success Metrics:**
- Homepage bounce rate target: under 45–55% for qualified local traffic.
- Scroll depth target: at least 60% reach services section.
- Gallery engagement target: 35%+ interact with project proof.
- Contact conversion target: 3–6% submit consultation requests.
- Device parity: mobile conversion ≥ 80% of desktop.

### Final Wireframe Principle

Every section should answer one of five questions fast: Who are you? What do you do? Where do you work? Why should I trust you? How do I start a conversation?
