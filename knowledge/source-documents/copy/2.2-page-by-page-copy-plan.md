# Haven Creek Renovations — Page-by-Page Copy Plan

## 1. Core Copy Strategy

Haven Creek should not sound like a generic renovation contractor. The copy should position the company as a **trusted, hands-on renovation partner for rural and acreage homeowners** who care about property respect, continuity, craftsmanship, and a long-term working relationship.

The main emotional question the website must answer is:

> "Can I trust this person on my property?"

The ideal customer is cautious, established, rural, proof-led, and relationship-driven.

## 2. Brand Copy Positioning

**Category to Own:** Trusted phased renovations for rural properties.
**Core Message Territory:** One trusted contractor. Rural property respect. Quality work from planning through completion.

**Brand Feeling:** Warm / Trustworthy / Rural / Grounded / Simple / Elegant / Durable / Calm / Clear. Avoid corporate, cheap, hard-sell, overly modern, urban, confusing, overdesigned.

## 3. Voice Rules

**Use:** Calm. Direct. Warm. Grounded. Mature. Respectful. Practical.
**Avoid:** Best. Luxury. Top-rated. Fast and affordable. Book now. Claim your quote. Limited spots. Dream home. Stress-free magic.

## 4. Conversion Strategy

Primary CTA: **Request a Consultation**. Secondary: **View Our Work / Talk Through Your Project / Discuss Your Property Improvement**. No fixed pricing. Pricing language should feel thoughtful, not evasive.

# Page-by-Page Copy Plan

## Page 1: Home

The Home page must create confidence within the first few seconds. Trust-first, not service-list-first.

**Copy Arc:**
1. **Hero** — Lead with rural renovation trust. CTA: Request a Consultation. Secondary: View Our Work. Microcopy reduces pressure.
2. **Trust Promise** — Hands-on involvement, fewer handoffs, property respect, continuity.
3. **Services Preview** — Interior Finishing first (most refined craft language), Exterior Finishing & Repairs, Decking.
4. **Full-Circle Approach** — One contractor across planning, structural, finishing, exterior, design.
5. **Project Gallery Preview** — Real photos. Captions explain the work, not just label it.
6. **Service Areas** — Bragg Creek, Rocky View County, Bearspaw, Water Valley.
7. **Final CTA** — Calm consultation invitation.

**Home Page Copy Principle:** The Home page should feel like a quiet handshake, not a sales pitch.

## Page 2: About

Build personal trust. Not generic company history.

**Copy Arc:**
1. Opening Position — Hands-on rural renovation partner. Lead with working style.
2. Working Philosophy — Meaning behind "The experience of quality. The quality of experience."
3. Property Respect — Speak directly to rural privacy, land, routines, access, family environment, trust.
4. Hands-On Continuity — Personal and accountable.
5. Long-Term Relationship — Suited for phased improvements over time.
6. CTA — Talk through your property improvement.

**About Page Copy Principle:** Make the owner feel trustworthy before making the company sound impressive.

## Page 3: Services Overview

Stay focused. Three services only.

**Hierarchy:**
1. **Interior Finishing** — Lead service. Highest craft value. Detailed, refined, careful, complete.
2. **Exterior Finishing & Repairs** — Protection, durability, maintenance, weather exposure, rural property care.
3. **Decking** — Outdoor living, daily enjoyment, long-term durability, connection to property.

**Copy Arc:** Services Intro → Three Service Cards (What is it? Why does it matter? What does the homeowner gain?) → Full-Circle Support → Custom Quote Explanation → CTA.

**Services Page Copy Principle:** Simplify the decision and make Haven Creek feel focused, not limited.

**Thank You Page Copy Principle:** The visitor should feel calmer after submitting than before.

# Copy System Rules Across the Site

1. **Proof Comes Before Persuasion** — Let project images, captions, scope notes, and process clarity do the heavy lifting.
2. **Use Rural Language Carefully** — Tone should feel rural and grounded, not rustic, rough, or cliché. No cowboy language, no farmhouse clichés.
3. **Keep the Website Quietly Premium** — The logo already suggests maturity. Copy should match: restrained, clear, confident.
4. **Make "Quality" Specific** — Define through: details checked, work fitting together properly, fewer handoffs, property respect, durable results, calm communication, finished work that feels complete.
5. **Never Compete on Cheapness** — No "free quote" energy. No "affordable contractor" positioning. No urgency gimmicks.

# Final Strategic Copy Direction

The entire website should be built around one central belief:

**For rural homeowners, renovation is not just about what gets built. It is about trusting the person doing the work on the property they value.**

That is the emotional center of Haven Creek Renovations.
