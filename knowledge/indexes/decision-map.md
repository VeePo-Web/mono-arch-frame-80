# Decision Map — If User Asks X, Consult Y

Quick lookup for common request patterns. Always read **priority-hierarchy.md** alongside the listed docs.

## "Change the colors / palette"
1. Open `source-documents/ui-ux/1.3-client-design-preferences.md` § 5 Color Preferences (client-stated palette)
2. Open `source-documents/brand-identity/1.5-brand-identity-north-star.md` § Color Logic (operating rules)
3. Cross-check `source-documents/visual-design/2.3-full-visual-design-plan.md` § 6 (color feeling: materials, not paint chips)
4. Confirm: warm off-white, charcoal, muted olive/natural green, warm neutrals. **No** bright lime, neon, cold blue, gold luxury, harsh black.

## "Update the headline / hero copy"
1. `source-documents/copy/2.2-page-by-page-copy-plan.md` § Page 1 Home (copy arc)
2. `source-documents/wireframes/2.1-conversion-wireframe-spec.md` § Hero Section Requirements (35-char headline, 74-char subhead spec)
3. `source-documents/brand-identity/1.5-brand-identity-north-star.md` § Pillar headlines (10 options per pillar)
4. `source-documents/brand-identity/1.2-strategic-narrative-and-brand.md` § Premium Website Hero Message

## "Add a new section / new page"
1. `source-documents/ui-ux/1.3-client-design-preferences.md` § 8 Navigation and Page Structure (what client did and did NOT select)
2. `source-documents/wireframes/2.1-conversion-wireframe-spec.md` (architecture)
3. `source-documents/copy/2.2-page-by-page-copy-plan.md` (copy arc per page)
4. If the client did not select it, either fold the content into an existing page OR get explicit user approval.

## "Style the buttons / CTAs"
1. `source-documents/wireframes/2.1-conversion-wireframe-spec.md` § Buttons + CTA Specifications
2. `source-documents/brand-identity/1.5-brand-identity-north-star.md` § CTA Color
3. `source-documents/brand-identity/1.2-strategic-narrative-and-brand.md` § Conversion Strategy (allowed CTA language; banned CTA language)
4. Default: deep natural green fill, off-white text, rounded but not pill, subtle hover lift.

## "Change the tone / make it more X"
1. `source-documents/brand-identity/1.5-brand-identity-north-star.md` § Voice Traits + Lexicon + Replacement Phrases
2. `source-documents/brand-identity/1.2-strategic-narrative-and-brand.md` § Brand Voice
3. `source-documents/copy/2.2-page-by-page-copy-plan.md` § Voice Rules
4. Forbidden: cheap, fast, luxury (without proof), book now, claim your quote, dream home, stress-free magic.

## "Add testimonials / reviews"
1. `source-documents/ui-ux/1.3-client-design-preferences.md` (client did NOT select Testimonials page)
2. `source-documents/wireframes/2.1-conversion-wireframe-spec.md` § Testimonial Strategy ("if testimonials do not exist yet, use project proof captions instead")
3. Decision: no dedicated Testimonials page. Captions on project gallery cards. Real reviews integrated into proof modules only when they exist.

## "Add an FAQ / pricing tier / quote calculator"
1. `source-documents/ui-ux/1.3-client-design-preferences.md` (client did NOT select FAQ or Quote Request)
2. `source-documents/brand-identity/1.5-brand-identity-north-star.md` § Non-Negotiables #8: no fixed pricing
3. Decision: no FAQ page, no pricing table. Allowed: a single "Why custom quotes?" microcopy line on Contact or Services.

## "Style the project gallery"
1. `source-documents/copy/2.2-page-by-page-copy-plan.md` § Page 8 Project Gallery (card structure)
2. `source-documents/visual-design/2.3-full-visual-design-plan.md` § 9 Project Gallery Direction
3. `source-documents/brand-identity/1.5-brand-identity-north-star.md` § Pillar 3 (craftsmanship proof)
4. `source-documents/strategy/1.1-market-dominance-blueprint.md` § Project Gallery SEO Format
5. Each card: project type, location, scope, challenge, result, why it mattered.

## "Add motion / animations"
1. `source-documents/visual-design/2.3-full-visual-design-plan.md` § 14 Motion Feeling
2. `source-documents/wireframes/2.1-conversion-wireframe-spec.md` § Progressive Reveal + Micro-Interactions
3. Defaults: subtle fade-up, 180–280ms, no parallax (or extremely restrained), motion slows visitor down to build trust.

## "Make it feel more premium / luxury"
1. `source-documents/brand-identity/1.5-brand-identity-north-star.md` § Conflict Resolutions ("premium without proof = no")
2. `source-documents/visual-design/2.3-full-visual-design-plan.md` § 2 Visual Positioning (refined rural, not luxury)
3. Translate: instead of "luxury," express premium through restraint, generous whitespace, mature serif, real photography, calm pacing.

## "Add urgency / scarcity / limited spots"
1. `source-documents/brand-identity/1.5-brand-identity-north-star.md` § Dealbreakers
2. `source-documents/wireframes/2.1-conversion-wireframe-spec.md` § Urgency/scarcity ("Do not use fake urgency")
3. Decision: hard no. Replace with calm invitation language.

## "Build a contact form"
1. `source-documents/strategy/1.1-market-dominance-blueprint.md` § Contact Form Strategy (10 fields)
2. `source-documents/copy/2.2-page-by-page-copy-plan.md` § Page 11 Contact (form fields + copy arc)
3. `source-documents/wireframes/2.1-conversion-wireframe-spec.md` § Form Fields (focus, error, success states)
4. Required field that captures the brand: "Is this part of phased improvements over time?"

## "I want to feel like the user before deciding"
1. `source-documents/personas/1.4-steady-steward-sam-persona.md` § A Day in Their Life + Landing Page Handoff Brief
2. Apply the test: "Would Steady Steward Sam feel safer reaching out after seeing this?"
