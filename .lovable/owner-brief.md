## What this is

We rebuilt the top bar and the menu page to feel like a studio site, not a contractor template. Same five pages, calmer, faster, more confident.

---

## What changed, in plain English

- **The top bar is the same shape on phone and desktop.** Logo on the left. Call us, "Get a Free Quote", menu icon on the right. No page links scattered across the bar.
- **The menu icon opens a full-screen evergreen page.** Five large serif route names cascade in one after another. One way in, one way out.
- **The bar fades in a quiet cream wash as you scroll.** It tucks away when you scroll down, returns the moment you scroll up. It never gets in the way of the work.
- **The logo crossfades** from cream over the hero photo to dark after scroll, so it always stays readable.
- **The hamburger is a refined two-line glyph** that morphs into an X when the menu is open. The lines slide on hover — a small, deliberate touch.

## Why it feels like Fly4Me — but more Haven Creek

The reference is Fly4Me.ca. What we kept:

- The restraint. One menu trigger, one CTA, no clutter.
- The discipline of a single entry point — every visitor takes the same calm path through the site.
- The cinematic full-screen menu instead of a tiny dropdown.

What we changed to make it ours:

- Evergreen on cream, not cold tech-blue. Warmer, more residential.
- Serif route names, not sans. It reads as craft, not software.
- Motion is slower and softer — 520ms instead of 280ms — so the site feels considered, not snappy.
- Photography drives the page, not screenshots. Your work is the hero.

---

## Performance — what "instant" means here

Targets for this site:

- The first paint of the home page happens in under 1.2 seconds.
- The menu opens within a single frame of being tapped — 80 to 140 milliseconds.
- Nothing on the page shifts or jumps while it loads.
- No spinners. Anywhere.

We get there by preparing the menu in the browser's spare moments before you ever tap it, by quietly preloading each page as soon as your cursor moves toward it, and by only animating the things browsers can move cheaply.

---

## What we deliberately kept out

Every removal is a decision.

- **No dropdowns or mega-menus.** They make small businesses look corporate.
- **No social icons in the header.** They send people away from the page they're on.
- **No "Menu" word next to the icon.** The icon is the word.
- **No second CTA in the bar.** One ask: "Get a Free Quote."
- **No testimonials in the navigation.** Trust is earned on the page, not in the chrome.
